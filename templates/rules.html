Standard Operating Procedure for Image Capture

To guarantee millimeter-level accuracy during AI extraction, the computer vision model relies on a strict geometric relationship between the camera and the structure. Please adhere to the following 5 golden rules before uploading an image.

  • 1

    1:1 Square Crop (Crucial)

    Avoid uploading ultra-wide panoramic or tall photos directly. The model processes images in a standard square (256x256). Pre-cropping your photo to a 1:1 aspect ratio on your phone/PC prevents the math algorithm from vertically or horizontally "squashing" the defect.

  • 2

    Orthogonal Framing

    Hold the camera perfectly parallel to the wall or floor. Capturing photos at downward or sideways angles introduces perspective distortion, which breaks the dynamic pinhole scaling mathematics.

  • 3

    Accurate Standoff Distance

    The system does not automatically detect distance. You must measure the distance from the camera lens to the wall (e.g., 30 cm) and select the corresponding option from the dropdown menu on the dashboard before analyzing.

  • 4

    Uniform Lighting

    Ensure the surface is evenly lit. Heavy cast shadows from trees or scaffolding can mimic structural fissures, causing false positives in the U-Net segmentation layer.

  • 5

    Resolution Guidelines

    You do not need massive 4K images. Because the pipeline dynamically downsizes imagery for high-speed inference, a standard focused 1024x1024 or 720p image provides perfect clarity for extraction.
